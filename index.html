01 — About

Building at the intersection
of full-stack & AI.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, building on a Diploma in Information Technology from Parul University. My work sits between full-stack architecture and applied AI — designing systems with MERN, Next.js, and FastAPI, then wiring them to intelligent pipelines powered by Gemini and Python AST analysis.

I care equally about system design and interface craft, and I like measuring what I build — every project here ships with real deployment and real Lighthouse performance data, not just a description of what it does.

Outside of solo projects, I led an engineering team at Hack-A-Throne 2025 as Team Leader, and helped build RentFlow through to the offline final round of the Odoo × GCET Hackathon 2026.
